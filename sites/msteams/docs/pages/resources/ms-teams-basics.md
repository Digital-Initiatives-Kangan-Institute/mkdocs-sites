# Microsoft Teams Meeting Basics Worksheet

## What You Will Learn

- Join a Teams meeting
- Use your microphone and camera
- Raise your hand and use reactions
- Use meeting chat and view participants
- Create and run your own meeting

> **Important:** Do not worry if you make a mistake. This worksheet is for practising. Ask your teacher or a classmate for help when you need it.

## Task 1: Find and Open Microsoft Teams

**Goal:** Become comfortable opening Teams.

1. Find Microsoft Teams on your computer.
2. Open Microsoft Teams.
3. Check that you are signed in.
4. Find your profile picture or initials.
5. Find Chat.
6. Find Calendar.
7. Find Calls, if it is available.

**Check your progress**

- Teams is open
- I can find Chat
- I can find Calendar

## Task 2: Join a Meeting

**Goal:** Learn how to enter a Teams meeting.

1. Open Calendar in Teams.
2. Find the meeting provided by your teacher.
3. Select the meeting.
4. Choose **Join**.
5. Before entering, look at the meeting screen.
6. Turn your camera off.
7. Turn your microphone off (mute).
8. Select **Join now**.

**Check your progress**

- I joined the meeting
- My microphone was muted when I joined
- My camera was off when I joined

### Task 2.1: Pre-Meeting Settings

**Goal:** Check and set your camera, microphone, sound and background before joining.

1. Open the meeting and choose **Join** to open the pre-join screen.
2. Toggle the camera on.
3. Toggle the microphone on.
4. Speak and check that the sound-level indicator moves, then mute it again.
5. Select **Computer audio**, then open **Audio settings**.
6. Check that the correct speaker and microphone are selected.
7. Set the speaker volume to a comfortable level.
8. Open **Effects and avatars**.
9. Choose a background effect.

**Check your progress**

- I toggled my camera on and off
- I checked and muted my microphone
- I selected the correct speaker and microphone
- I checked my background
- I was ready before selecting **Join now**

## Task 9: Managing Gallery and View Settings

**Goal:** Choose a meeting view that helps you see people and shared work.

1. Join your teacher’s practice meeting.
2. Select **View**, or **More actions > View**, in the meeting controls.
3. Choose **Gallery** to see the people in the meeting.
4. Open **Gallery size** and choose another available size. Notice what changes.
5. Hover over your teacher’s video. Select **More options > Pin for me**.
6. Select **More options > Unpin for me** to undo the change.
7. When your teacher shares some work, select **View > Focus on content**. Return to Gallery afterwards.

> **Tip:** Pin for me changes only your view. If an option is missing, ask your teacher to help.

**Check your progress**

- I selected Gallery
- I tried a different gallery size
- I pinned and unpinned a video
- I changed my view to focus on shared work

## Task 10: Sharing Your Screen

**Goal:** Show your work to others and stop sharing when you finish.

1. Open a practice document provided by your teacher.
2. Close private documents, messages and browser tabs before sharing.
3. In the practice meeting, wait until your teacher asks you to share.
4. Select **Share** in the meeting controls.
5. Choose **Window** and select your practice document. This shares only that window. **Screen** shares your whole screen.
6. Ask a classmate: “Can you see my practice document?”
7. Point to one part of your work and explain it.
8. Move your pointer to the top of the shared screen to find the presenter toolbar. Select **Stop sharing**.
9. Ask your classmate to check that your work is no longer being shared.

> **Important:** Share only practice material, not passwords or personal information. If Share is unavailable, ask your teacher to make you a presenter.

**Check your progress**

- I checked for private information before sharing
- I shared the correct window
- Another person could see my work
- I stopped sharing

## Task 11: Changing Status and Status Messages

**Goal:** Tell others when you are available and practise setting a timed message.

### Change Your Status

1. Select your profile picture or initials at the top of Teams.
2. Select your current status and choose **Be right back**.
3. Open your status again and select **Duration**.
4. Choose when the status should reset under **Reset status after**, then select **Done**.

### Set a Status Message

5. From your profile, select **Set status message**.
6. Type a practice message: “I am practising Teams with my class.”
7. Select **Show when people message me**. Choose a time under **Clear status message after**, then select **Done**.

### Practise Scheduling an Out-of-Office Message

8. With your teacher, open **Set status message > Schedule out of office**.
9. Turn on automatic replies and type a practice message: “I am away for a class activity.” Leave replies to people outside your organisation off.
10. Select **Send replies only during a time period**. Enter a future start and end time agreed with your teacher.
11. Show your teacher the settings. Do not select **Save** unless your teacher approves. Saving also turns on automatic replies in Outlook.
12. Close without saving. If you saved an approved practice schedule, turn off automatic replies and select **Save** when practice finishes.
13. Clear your practice status message and select **Reset status** from your status menu.

**Check your progress**

- I changed my status and set a reset time
- I wrote a status message and chose when it clears
- I practised choosing an out-of-office time period
- I removed my practice settings

## Task 12: Creating a Chat with Multiple Users

**Goal:** Start a group conversation with two classmates.

1. Agree with your teacher which two classmates to include.
2. Open Chat in Teams and select **New message**.
3. Select the down arrow beside **To** to show **Group name**.
4. Name the group “Teams Chat Practice”.
5. In **To**, type each classmate’s name and select the correct person from the suggestions.
6. Check both names before sending anything.
7. Type “Hello! This is our practice group chat.” Select **Send**.
8. Ask each classmate to send a short reply. Read both replies.
9. Select **View and add participants** to check who is in the group.
10. Open another chat, then find **Teams Chat Practice** again in your chat list.

> **Important:** Everyone in the group can read the messages. Keep your practice messages friendly and do not share private information.

**Check your progress**

- I created and named a group chat
- I included the correct classmates
- I sent a message and read replies
- I checked the group members
- I found my group chat again
